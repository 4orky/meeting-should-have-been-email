One joke, in one line, about a meeting that should have been an email. The program prints the entry:

> One-line agenda, ninety-minute meeting -- and the follow-up email was just the agenda, forwarded.

## Why this line

- **Lands on its own, no setup.** Three beats — one-line agenda, ninety-minute meeting, the follow-up email — and the punchline is the last word: the meeting's entire output was its own input, merely forwarded.
- **Fresh turn.** No "could have been an email" sigh, no meeting-about-the-meeting trope, no "circle back" cliche — the joke is the loop itself, told as a ledger.
- **Every word earns its place.** 97 characters. "Just" carries the whole shrug; "forwarded" is the twist. Nothing in the line is spare.
- **Original.** Twelve hand-written candidates were scored against the brief and this one won; a second pass tightened the winning draft from 105 characters to 97.
